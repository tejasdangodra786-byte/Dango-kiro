# Worksheet 14 — Session-by-Session Treatment Map

**Phase 2–6 — Treatment Planning & Tracking**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 1 (the arc of treatment), Ch. 11 (EFT nine steps / three stages), Ch. 8 (CBCT treatment plan), Ch. 12 (Solution-Focused), Ch. 2 (PAIRS curriculum format).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose:** A single-page overview that maps every session to its phase, goal, worksheets, and techniques — so you always know where you are in the treatment arc. Fill in actual dates and notes as therapy proceeds.
- **Format:** Clinician planning tool. Review before each session; update after.
- **Flexibility:** The *Handbook* does not prescribe a rigid session count. This map shows a typical 16-session course. Compress (brief/solution-focused) or extend (EFT, object-relations, complex issues) as needed. Sessions can be reordered when special issues or crises arise.

## EFT Nine Steps Mapped to Sessions (reference)

| EFT Step | Description | Typical sessions |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| Step 1 | Assessment — alliance, cycle identification, attachment | Sessions 1–4 |
| Step 2 | Identify the negative interactional cycle | Sessions 4–5 |
| Step 3 | Access primary / unacknowledged emotions | Sessions 5–7 |
| Step 4 | Reframe the problem as the cycle + attachment needs | Sessions 4–6 |
| Step 5 | Promote identification of disowned attachment needs/fears | Sessions 8–10 |
| Step 6 | Promote acceptance of partner's emerging experience | Sessions 9–11 |
| Step 7 | Facilitate expression of needs/wants; bonding events | Sessions 10–12 |
| Step 8 | Facilitate new solutions to old problems | Sessions 13–14 |
| Step 9 | Consolidate new positions and cycles | Sessions 14–16 |
