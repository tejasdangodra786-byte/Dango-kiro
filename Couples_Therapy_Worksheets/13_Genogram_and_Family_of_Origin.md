# Worksheet 13 — Genogram & Family-of-Origin Assessment

**Phase 1 — Assessment · Individual Sessions (2–3)**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 7 (Bowen Family Systems: multigenerational transmission, differentiation, triangles, cutoffs), Ch. 2 (PAIRS individual histories: family conditioning, models from the parental marriage), Ch. 9 (Object Relations: internalized models).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose:** Map each partner's multigenerational family patterns. Bowen theory (Ch. 7) holds that couple dynamics are shaped by patterns transmitted across generations — differentiation levels, triangles, emotional cutoffs, and the model of the parental marriage. The PAIRS chapter similarly gathers "family conditioning, early beginnings, models from the parental marriage."
- **Format:** Complete in **individual sessions**, one per partner. Draw the genogram together with the partner on paper/whiteboard, then explore the patterns.
- **Why it helps the couple:** Partners often repeat or react against what they learned at home. Seeing the pattern reduces blame ("this is a pattern I inherited, not a character flaw") and opens change.

## Bringing it to the couple

In the joint feedback session (Worksheet 03), help each partner see:
- "The way you each react in conflict makes sense given where you came from."
- How the two family systems/patterns **collide or combine** to create the couple's cycle.
- This reframes blame into understanding — a key non-blaming move the book emphasizes throughout.
