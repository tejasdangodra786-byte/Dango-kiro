# Worksheet 10 — Presenting Problems Checklist & Problem Map

**Phase 1 — Assessment · Session 1–2**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 1 (common presentations), Section III (special issues: illness, abuse, aggression, addiction, infidelity, spiritual, same-sex, socioeconomic, divorce), Ch. 3 (early-marriage adjustment areas), Ch. 8 (functional assessment of the target problem).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose:** Systematically identify *all* the problems a couple brings — not just the one they lead with. Couples often present a "surface" complaint while a deeper or more dangerous issue sits underneath. This checklist ensures nothing is missed and feeds the functional assessment (Worksheet 11) and formulation (Worksheet 03).
- **Format:** Give **each partner their own copy** to complete independently, then compare in session. Divergence between the two lists is itself clinically meaningful.
- **Rank:** After checking all that apply, have each partner rank their **top 3** by distress.
- **Safety:** Any item in the "Safety-sensitive" section triggers the matching module in Worksheet 07 and may change the treatment sequence.

## Part A — Communication & Conflict

- [ ] We argue frequently / can't stop fighting
- [ ] We avoid conflict / never resolve anything
- [ ] One of us criticizes; the other withdraws
- [ ] Contempt, name-calling, or disrespect
- [ ] Defensiveness — no one takes responsibility
- [ ] Stonewalling / silent treatment
- [ ] We can't talk about hard topics without it blowing up
- [ ] Poor listening / feeling unheard
- [ ] Mind-reading / assuming the worst

## Part B — Emotional Connection & Intimacy

- [ ] We feel like roommates / have grown apart
- [ ] Loss of affection or warmth
- [ ] Not feeling loved, valued, or appreciated
- [ ] Loneliness within the relationship
- [ ] Loss of trust
- [ ] Emotional distance / walls up
- [ ] One pursues closeness; the other needs space

## Part C — Sex & Physical Intimacy

- [ ] Mismatched desire / frequency
- [ ] Loss of sexual interest
- [ ] Sexual dissatisfaction
- [ ] Sexual difficulties / dysfunction
- [ ] Impact of past sexual trauma (→ Worksheet 07, Module B)
- [ ] Pornography or compulsive sexual behavior concerns

## Part D — Roles, Household & Parenting

- [ ] Unfair division of household labor
- [ ] Disagreements about parenting
- [ ] Child/adolescent problems straining the couple (→ Ch. 4–5)
- [ ] Blended-family / step-parenting conflict
- [ ] Caregiving burden (child, elder, or ill partner)
- [ ] Different values about work vs. family time

## Part E — Money & Life Logistics

- [ ] Financial stress / insufficient income (→ Worksheet 07, Module I)
- [ ] Conflict over spending / saving / debt
- [ ] Power imbalance tied to earning
- [ ] Disagreement about major decisions (moving, career, kids)

## Part F — External & Contextual Stressors

- [ ] Extended-family / in-law conflict
- [ ] Cultural or ethnic differences causing friction
- [ ] Religious / spiritual differences (→ Worksheet 07, Module F)
- [ ] Minority stress / lack of acceptance (→ Worksheet 07, Module H)
- [ ] Work stress, unemployment, or relocation
- [ ] Health / chronic illness / disability (→ Worksheet 07, Module A)
- [ ] Grief / recent loss

## Part G — Safety-Sensitive (always review carefully)

- [ ] Physical aggression / violence (→ Worksheet 07, Module C) ⚠️
- [ ] Fear of partner / coercive control ⚠️
- [ ] Emotional / verbal / financial abuse ⚠️
- [ ] Addiction — alcohol, drugs, gambling, etc. (→ Module D) ⚠️
- [ ] Infidelity — discovered or suspected (→ Module E)
- [ ] Mental-health crisis / self-harm risk ⚠️
- [ ] One or both considering separation/divorce (→ Module G)
