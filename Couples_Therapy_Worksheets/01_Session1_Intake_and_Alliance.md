# Worksheet 01 — First Session: Joint Intake, Safety Screen & Alliance

**Phase 1 — Assessment & Alliance · Session 1**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 1 (Setting the Stage), Ch. 2 (PAIRS premarital assessment), Ch. 8 (CBCT functional assessment), Ch. 16–17 (safety screening).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose of Session 1:** establish the alliance, understand the couple's shared story, run a safety screen, and set the frame — *not* to solve problems or assign skills yet.
- **Format:** Conjoint (both partners present). If your setting allows, book a **longer first session** (90–120 min). The PAIRS chapter stresses that the standard 50-minute hour "cannot build the trust, connectedness, and collaboration" an initial inquiry needs.
- **Stance:** Warm, curious, multidirectionally partial (both partners feel joined). Reframe "who's at fault" into "what's the pattern." Keep a non-blaming attitude throughout.
- **Fill it in together** on a shared surface for Parts B–D; complete Part A (safety) using your clinical interview and, if needed, brief separate check-ins.
- **End the session** by setting up the individual sessions (Worksheet 02) and explaining your no-secrets / confidentiality policy.

## Part B — The Relationship Story (joint)

Ask the couple to tell their story together. These prompts are adapted from the PAIRS premarital joint-interview questions and apply to committed couples at any life-cycle stage.

1. **How did you meet?** _______________________________________________
2. **What drew you to each other?** ______________________________________
3. **What made you decide to commit to this relationship?** ________________
4. **What have been the high points of your relationship so far?** __________
5. **What life-cycle stage are you in?** (circle) premarital · newly committed · young children · adolescents at home · older/"empty nest" · later life
6. **What major transitions or stressors are you facing now?** (work, finances, health, children, extended family, moves) _______________________
7. **What are family responses to your relationship — and how do you each react to the other's family?** _______________________________________
