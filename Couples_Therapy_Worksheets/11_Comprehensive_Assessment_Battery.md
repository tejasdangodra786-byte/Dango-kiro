# Worksheet 11 — Comprehensive Couple Assessment Battery

**Phase 1 — Assessment · Sessions 1–3**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 2 (PAIRS multi-session assessment format), Ch. 8 (CBCT functional assessment & assessment domains), Ch. 11 (EFT Step 1 assessment of cycle, attachment insecurities, longings & needs), Ch. 7 (Bowen differentiation), Ch. 24 (what research says distinguishes well-functioning couples).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose:** A structured, multi-domain assessment covering every area the *Handbook* says matters before you plan treatment: the couple's history, the interaction cycle, each partner's individual functioning, attachment, strengths, and risk. The CBCT chapter is explicit: *"Before any clinical theory or technique is applied, a functional assessment should be conducted to guide treatment planning."*
- **Format:** This is a **clinician-administered battery** spread across the first 2–3 sessions (joint + individual). The PAIRS model uses a joint interview, then two individual interviews, then a joint feedback session — this worksheet supports all of those.
- **Scope:** Not every domain needs equal depth for every couple. Prioritize based on the presenting problems (Worksheet 10).
- **Output:** Findings feed directly into the formulation and treatment plan (Worksheet 03).
