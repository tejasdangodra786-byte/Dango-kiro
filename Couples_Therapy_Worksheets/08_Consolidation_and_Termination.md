# Worksheet 08 — Consolidation, Relapse Prevention & Termination

**Phase 6 — Consolidation & Termination · Final 2–3 Sessions**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 2 (PAIRS: rituals, sustaining relationships, gratitude), Ch. 11 (EFT: consolidation stage, new positions), Ch. 24 (What the research tells us), Ch. 25 (concluding thoughts).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose:** Consolidate gains, help the couple internalize new patterns, build relapse prevention, and end the therapy relationship well. In EFT terms, this is the consolidation stage where new, more secure interactional positions are stabilized and the couple's new "story" of their relationship is reinforced.
- **Format:** Joint sessions, usually the final 2–3. Spread termination over more than one session so it isn't abrupt.
- **Stance:** Shift authority back to the couple — they are now the experts on their own relationship. Highlight what *they* did, not what you did.
- **Review material:** Pull out the goals (Worksheet 03), the cycle map, and completed tracking sheets (Worksheet 09) to review progress concretely.

## Part F — Ending the Therapy Relationship

- **Acknowledge the work and the ending.** Name what the couple accomplished.
- **Normalize mixed feelings** about ending.
- **Confirm the door is open** for future tune-up sessions.
- **Provide resources:** books, PAIRS or skills courses, support resources relevant to any special-issue modules used.
- **Clinician reflection / discharge summary:**
  - Presenting problem: _________________________________________
  - Approach used: ____________________________________________
  - Goals met / partially met / not met: ___________________________
  - Recommendations / referrals: _________________________________
  - Follow-up plan: ____________________________________________

## Session close-out

- [ ] Goals reviewed with concrete before/after ratings
- [ ] Relapse-prevention plan written and given to the couple
- [ ] Sustaining rituals committed to
- [ ] Resources and tune-up criteria provided
- [ ] Discharge summary documented
- [ ] Final session rating (Worksheet 09)

> **Research reminder (Ch. 24):** Positive gains from effective couples work (especially EFT) tend to be stable with relatively low relapse; a clear relapse-prevention plan supports maintenance. Reinforce the couple's own agency in sustaining change.
