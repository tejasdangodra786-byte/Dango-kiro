# Worksheet 15 — Weekly Session Tracking & Homework Log

**Ongoing · Every Session**
*Source basis: Harway (Ed.), Handbook of Couples Therapy — Ch. 8 (CBCT between-session practice), Ch. 12 (solution-focused scaling), Ch. 2 (PAIRS exercises & practice between sessions), Ch. 24 (process tracking).*

---

## How to use this worksheet (clinician instructions)

- **Purpose:** Keep a continuous record of session-by-session progress and between-session practice. The *Handbook*'s skills-based chapters (CBCT, PAIRS) depend on practice between sessions; the solution-focused chapter uses scaling to track movement.
- **Format:** Photocopy/duplicate the tracking table for each session. Review the previous week's homework at the **start** of each session; assign new homework at the **end**.
- **Why it matters:** Reviewing homework communicates that the real work happens between sessions, reinforces accountability, and gives you process data to adjust the plan.
- **Keep both:** a per-session rating (quick scale) and a homework log.

## D — Homework Menu (pick from these by phase)

**Phase 1–2 (Assessment/Formulation):**
- Each partner notices and records one thing they appreciate about the other daily
- Observe (don't change) when the negative cycle shows up; note the trigger

**Phase 3 (Communication/De-escalation):**
- Daily 15-min Speaker–Listener check-in on a positive topic (Worksheet 04)
- Convert 3 blame-thoughts into I-messages
- Practice the agreed Time-Out once if reactivity rises

**Phase 4 (Restructuring):**
- One "vulnerability conversation" using the enactment script (Worksheet 05)
- Each partner performs ≥ _____ caring behaviors/day from partner's list (Worksheet 06)
- Complete 2 thought records (Worksheet 06)
- Daily bonding ritual (20-sec hug, check-in, gratitude)

**Phase 5 (Special issues):**
- Module-specific practice (e.g., transparency check-ins for infidelity; trigger log for trauma; relapse-prevention steps for addiction)

**Phase 6 (Consolidation):**
- Practice recovering from the cycle on your own and report back
- Implement the sustaining rituals from Worksheet 08
