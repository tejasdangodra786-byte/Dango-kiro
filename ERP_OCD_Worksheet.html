How to use this sheet  /  Ise kaise bharna hai

  1. List your obsessions. Baar-baar aane wale khayal, images, dar ya doubts (jaise "haath gande hain", "darwaza khula reh gaya") column 2 mein likho.
  2. Write the trigger. Column 3 mein likho yeh khayal kis cheez se shuru hota hai — jagah, cheez, time, person ya situation (public toilet, gas stove, number 13).
  3. List the compulsion / ritual. Column 4 mein likho khayal ke baad aap kya karte ho — haath dhona, check karna, counting, praying, reassurance. Mental rituals bhi likho.
  4. Rate distress (SUDS 0–100). Column 5 mein likho ritual na karo to kitni tension hogi — 0 (bilkul nahi) se 100 (sabse zyada). Neeche scale dekho.
  5. Avoidance. Column 6 mein likho — kya aap us trigger/situation se bachte ho (avoid karte ho)? Haan / Nahi, aur kaise.
  6. Did you resist the compulsion? Column 7 mein likho — kya ritual rokne ki koshish ki? (Poora roka / Thoda roka / Nahi rok paaye).
  7. Frequency & time. Column 8 mein likho din bhar mein kitni baar aur kitna time (minutes) ritual mein jaata hai.

Golden rule / Yaad rakho: Pehle sabse aasaan (low SUDS) item se shuru karo, phir dheere-dheere higher SUDS wale items pe jao. Exposure ke time ritual mat karo — tension apne aap kam ho jayegi (habituation). Honestly bharo.
