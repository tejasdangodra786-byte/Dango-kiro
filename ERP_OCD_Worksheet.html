How to use this sheet  /  Ise kaise bharna hai

  1. List your obsessions. Jo bhi baar-baar aane wale khayal, images, dar ya doubts (jaise "haath gande hain", "darwaza khula reh gaya", "mujhse kisi ko nuksaan ho jayega") aate hain, unhe column 2 mein likho.
  2. Write the trigger. Column 3 mein likho ki yeh khayal kis cheez se shuru hota hai — koi jagah, cheez, time, person ya situation (jaise public toilet, gas stove, number 13, bachcha akela).
  3. List the compulsion / ritual. Column 4 mein likho ki us khayal ke baad aap kya karte ho taki tension kam ho — jaise haath dhona, check karna, counting, praying, reassurance maangna, mentally repeat karna. Mental rituals bhi likho.
  4. Rate the distress (SUDS 0–10). Column 5 mein likho ki agar aap woh ritual na karo to kitni tension / bechaini hogi — 0 (bilkul nahi) se 10 (bahut zyada). Neeche di gayi scale dekho.
  5. Avoidance. Column 6 mein likho — kya aap us trigger ya situation se bachte ho (avoid karte ho)? Haan / Nahi, aur kaise.
  6. Did you resist the compulsion? Column 7 mein likho — kya aapne ritual rokne ki koshish ki? (Haan poora roka / Thoda roka / Nahi rok paaye).
  7. Frequency & time. Column 8 mein likho din bhar mein kitni baar aur kitna time (minutes) us ritual mein jaata hai.

Golden rule / Yaad rakho: Pehle sabse aasaan (low SUDS) item se shuru karo, phir dheere-dheere higher SUDS wale items pe jao. Exposure ke time ritual mat karo — tension apne aap kam ho jayegi (habituation). Yeh koi test nahi hai, honestly bharo. Galat/sahi kuch nahi hota — sach likhna hi theek hona ka pehla kadam hai.
