TEST 1: VERBAL ADULT INTELLIGENCE SCALE (VAIS)

Author: D. Pershad (1977) | Age: 16+ years | Time: 30-40 min | Output: Verbal IQ

(VAIS ka har subtest ka scoring bilkul alag hai. Neeche har subtest ke liye exact items, exact scoring rules, time limits, aur discontinue rules diye gaye hain)

SUBTEST 1: INFORMATION

Basic Parameters

  • Number of Items: 29 questions
  • Scoring: 1 point per correct answer (no partial credit)
  • Maximum Score: 29
  • Start Point: Item 1 for all subjects
  • Discontinue: After 5 consecutive failures (5 zeros in a row)
  • Time Limit: No time limit per item (but don't wait more than 30 seconds for response)

Sample Items (from easy to hard):

5Suraj kis disha mein ugta hai? (In which direction does the sun rise?)East / Poorab0 or 1
10Bharat ki rajdhani kya hai? (What is the capital of India?)New Delhi0 or 1

Score 1 if: Subject gives the correct factual answer (wording may vary but fact must be correct).
Score 0 if: Answer is wrong, "I don't know," or no response after 30 seconds.
Acceptable variants: Different phrasing, regional language, synonyms are all acceptable IF factually correct.
Query (Q): If answer is vague/ambiguous, say "Tell me more" or "Explain what you mean" ONCE.
Q: "In which direction does the sun rise?"
• "East" = 1 point ✓
• "Poorab disha" = 1 point ✓
• "From that side" (points east) = Query, then if clarifies "East" = 1 ✓
• "West" = 0 points ✗
• "I don't know" = 0 points ✗
• No response after 30 seconds = 0 points ✗
SUBTEST 2: DIGIT SPAN

Basic Parameters

  • Two Parts: Digits Forward + Digits Backward
  • Maximum Score: Forward (max 9) + Backward (max 8) = 17
  • Rate of presentation: 1 digit per second (steady pace, no grouping)
  • Trials: 2 trials at each level
  • Discontinue: Both trials at a level failed
  • Score: Longest span passed (NOT total correct trials)

Scoring Formula:
Forward Score = Number of digits in the LONGEST series correctly repeated forward
Backward Score = Number of digits in the LONGEST series correctly repeated backward
TOTAL DIGIT SPAN = Forward Score + Backward Score

Pass criterion per trial: ALL digits must be in correct sequence. Even ONE digit wrong = fail that trial.
Pass criterion per level: Pass at least 1 out of 2 trials = move to next level.
Discontinue: BOTH trials at a level failed → Stop. Score = previous level's digit count.
Subject's performance (Forward):
Level 1 (3 digits): Trial 1 ✓ Trial 2 ✓ → Pass, move on
Level 2 (4 digits): Trial 1 ✓ Trial 2 ✗ → Pass (1 correct is enough), move on
Level 3 (5 digits): Trial 1 ✓ Trial 2 ✗ → Pass, move on
Level 4 (6 digits): Trial 1 ✗ Trial 2 ✓ → Pass, move on
Level 5 (7 digits): Trial 1 ✗ Trial 2 ✗ → STOP
Forward Score = 6 (longest level passed)

Subject's performance (Backward):
Level 1 (2 digits): Pass → Level 2 (3 digits): Pass → Level 3 (4 digits): Pass → Level 4 (5 digits): Both fail → STOP
Backward Score = 4

TOTAL DIGIT SPAN = 6 + 4 = 10
SUBTEST 3: ARITHMETIC

Basic Parameters

  • Number of Items: 14 problems of increasing difficulty
  • Scoring: 1 point per correct answer within time limit
  • Maximum Score: 14
  • Time Limits: Items 1-8 = 15 sec; Items 9-12 = 30 sec; Items 13-14 = 60 sec
  • Discontinue: After 4 consecutive failures
  • Rules: No pencil/paper; No finger counting; Oral presentation; Repeat item ONCE only if requested

Sample Items with Scoring:

Score 1 if: Numerically correct answer given within the time limit.
Score 0 if: Wrong answer, OR correct answer but AFTER time limit, OR no response.
Start timing: Immediately after you finish reading the problem.
Repeat: You may repeat the problem ONCE if subject requests. Restart timing after repetition.
No partial credit: Answer is either correct or incorrect.
SUBTEST 4: COMPREHENSION

Basic Parameters

  • Number of Items: 14 questions
  • Scoring: 0, 1, or 2 points per item (based on quality)
  • Maximum Score: 14 × 2 = 28
  • Discontinue: After 4 consecutive 0 scores
  • Time Limit: No formal time limit (but don't wait beyond 2 minutes)
  • Query: If answer is vague, say "Aur batayein" (Tell me more) ONCE

Sample Items with 2-1-0 Scoring Criteria:

Item: "Agar aapko sadak par ek stamp laga hua khat mila toh aap kya karenge?"
(What would you do if you found a stamped, addressed letter on the road?)

2 points: "Post it / Put it in a mailbox / Take it to the post office" (shows social responsibility AND practical solution)
1 point: "Return it to the person" / "Give it to someone" (partial solution; vague but shows intent)
0 points: "Open it and read it" / "Keep it" / "Throw it" / "Don't know" (no social judgment)
Item: "Kapde kyun dhoye jaate hain?" (Why do we wash clothes?)

2 points: "To remove dirt/germs AND to maintain hygiene/look clean" (two concepts: cleanliness + hygiene/appearance)
1 point: "To make them clean" / "Because they get dirty" (one concept only)
0 points: "Because mother says so" / "I don't know" (no understanding)
Item: "Vaada kyun nibhaana chahiye?" (Why should promises be kept?)

2 points: "To maintain trust" AND/OR "Society cannot function without keeping word" / "Moral obligation + practical consequence" (abstract reasoning + social understanding)
1 point: "Because it's wrong to break them" / "People won't trust you" (one reason, correct but limited)
0 points: "I don't know" / "Because God punishes" (superstitious/no reasoning)
Proverb Item: "'Lohe ko garam hai tab tak thoknaa' — iska matlab kya hai?"
(What does "strike while the iron is hot" mean?)

2 points: "Take advantage of opportunity when it arises" / "Act at the right time before it's too late" (abstract generalization)
1 point: "Don't waste time" / "Do things quickly" (correct direction but not precise)
0 points: "Iron is easier to bend when hot" (literal, concrete interpretation)
2 points = Response shows good judgment, abstract thinking, and considers multiple aspects.
1 point = Response is correct but limited, concrete, or addresses only one aspect.
0 points = Response is incorrect, irrelevant, or shows no understanding.

Query rule: If response is vague (could be 1 or 2), ask "Aur batayein?" (Tell me more) ONCE. Score based on total response (original + elaboration).

CALCULATING VIQ: Complete Procedure

VIQ = (TQInformation + TQDigit Span + TQArithmetic + TQComprehension) ÷ 4
1
Calculate Raw Scores: Information (0-29), Digit Span (0-17), Arithmetic (0-14), Comprehension (0-28)
2
Identify Age Band: 16-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35-39, 40-44, 45-49, 50-54, 55-59, 60+
3
Convert Each Raw Score to TQ: Use the norm table for that age band. Look up each raw score → get corresponding Test Quotient (TQ). Each TQ has Mean=100, SD=15.
4
Average the 4 TQs: VIQ = Sum of 4 TQs ÷ 4. Round to nearest whole number.
Patient: Male, Age 32, B.Com graduate
Raw Scores: Information=21, Digit Span=12, Arithmetic=10, Comprehension=20
From Norms Table (Age 30-34):
• Info Raw 21 → TQ = 102
• Digit Span Raw 12 → TQ = 108
• Arithmetic Raw 10 → TQ = 100
• Comprehension Raw 20 → TQ = 96
VIQ = (102 + 108 + 100 + 96) ÷ 4 = 406 ÷ 4 = 101.5 ≈ 102
Classification: Average Intelligence (90-109 range)

TEST 2: BHATIA'S BATTERY OF PERFORMANCE TESTS

Author: C.M. Bhatia (1955), Revised for PGI-BBD | Age: 11+ years | Time: 40-60 min | Output: Performance IQ

(Bhatia Battery mein 5 subtests hain. Short version mein sirf 2 (Block Design + Pass-Along). Scoring time-based hai — jitna jaldi sahi kiya utne zyada marks milte hain. Neeche exact scoring rules hain)

SUBTEST 1: KOH'S BLOCK DESIGN TEST

Materials

  • 16 cubes/blocks with faces colored: Red, White, Blue, Yellow, Red-White diagonal, Blue-Yellow diagonal
  • 10 design cards (arranged easy to hard)
  • Stopwatch

Basic Parameters

  • Designs 1-5: Use 4 blocks; Time limit = 2 minutes per design
  • Designs 6-10: Use 9 blocks; Time limit = 3 minutes per design
  • Maximum Score: 20
  • Demonstration: Examiner demonstrates Design 1 first, then disassembles and asks subject to do it

SUBTEST 2: ALEXANDER'S PASS-ALONG TEST

Materials & Task

A wooden tray/board with colored blocks that slide in grooves. Subject must rearrange blocks by SLIDING (not lifting) to match the target pattern shown.

  • Number of Problems: 8-10 (depending on version)
  • Maximum Score: 24
  • Scoring based on: Number of moves taken + time

Scoring Criteria:

SUBTESTS 3-5: Pattern Drawing, Immediate Memory, Picture Construction

Subtest 3: PATTERN DRAWING (Max = 19)

  • 5 geometric patterns to copy; scored 0-3 or 0-5 per pattern based on accuracy
  • Criteria: Proportions, angles, completeness, overall gestalt

Subtest 4: IMMEDIATE MEMORY (Max = 12)

  • Digit span forward task; score = longest span recalled correctly
  • Same scoring logic as VAIS Digit Span Forward portion

Subtest 5: PICTURE CONSTRUCTION (Max = 20)

  • 5 cut-up pictures to assemble; 4 marks each (time bonus for speed)
  • Correct + Fast = 4; Correct + Normal time = 3; Correct + Slow = 2; Partial = 1; Failed = 0

Critical Scoring Difference:
Similar Pairs: Score = BEST single trial out of 3 (max 10)
Dissimilar Pairs: Score = TOTAL of all 3 trials combined (max ~20)

TEST 4: NAHOR-BENSON TEST — Scoring Procedure

Step-by-Step Scoring

1
Give subject the test sheet with symbol-digit key at top
2
Demonstrate first 3-5 items as practice
3
Say "Shuru karo" and start stopwatch. Time limit = 90 seconds (or 120 sec per version)
4
Say "Ruko" at time limit. Mark where subject stopped.
5
Count Total Items Attempted (how far did they get?)
6
Count Errors (wrong symbols, skipped items, incomplete symbols)
7
Net Score = Total Attempted − Total Errors
8
Compare Net Score to age-education norms → Assign DRS (0, 2, or 3)
What counts as an Error:
• Wrong symbol drawn for that digit = 1 error
• Skipped item (left blank) = 1 error
• Symbol so poorly drawn it's unidentifiable = 1 error
• Self-corrections: If corrected properly, do NOT count as error

TEST 5: BENDER GESTALT (BVMGT) — Scoring the 12 Discriminators

(Bender Gestalt mein 9 designs copy karne ke baad, hum 12 tarah ki specific errors dekhte hain. Har error Present (1) ya Absent (0) score hoti hai. Total error count se brain dysfunction ka indication milta hai)

Step-by-Step Scoring Procedure

1
Collect all 9 reproductions from the subject
2
Lay them out together so you can see the full protocol
3
Go through EACH of the 12 discriminators one by one
4
For each discriminator: Is it present ANYWHERE in the 9 drawings? YES = 1, NO = 0
5
Each discriminator scored only ONCE regardless of how many designs show it
6
Total Error Score = Sum of all 12 (range 0-12)
7
Cutoff: 5+ errors = Suggestive of brain dysfunction

The 12 Discriminators — Exact Scoring Criteria:

#Error NameScore 1 (Present) IF:Score 0 (Absent) IF:
1RotationAny design rotated 80°+ from originalAll designs in correct orientation (minor tilts <80° OK)
2Overlapping DifficultyWhere two parts cross (designs 4,6,7), the overlap is distorted/simplified/avoidedOverlapping portions drawn correctly
3SimplificationAny design notably simplified (dots become lines; complex parts omitted)Complexity of designs maintained
4FragmentationAny design's unified gestalt broken into separate disconnected partsAll designs maintain their integrated form
5RetrogressionMarkedly primitive reproduction (substitution: lines for dots, scribbles for shapes)Age-appropriate reproduction quality
6PerseverationElements repeated beyond stimulus (extra dots/loops) OR previous design features intrude into currentNo extra repetitions; each design drawn independently
7Collision/Crowding2+ designs overlap each other on paper OR all jammed into one corner/areaDesigns reasonably spaced across page
8ImpotenceMultiple visible correction attempts showing subject KNOWS it's wrong but CANNOT fix itNo evidence of frustrated repeated attempts
9Motor IncoordinationNotably tremulous/irregular/uncontrolled lines throughout multiple designsLines are reasonably smooth and controlled
10Angulation DifficultyAngles consistently changed by 15°+ (acute becomes obtuse or vice versa)Angles approximately maintained
11CohesionParts that should touch are separated, OR parts that should be separate are fusedSpatial relationships between parts maintained
12Closure DifficultyFigures not closed (gaps >3mm) OR lines overshoot at junctionsFigures properly closed; junctions clean
Total BVMGT Error Score: Sum of 12 items (each 0 or 1) = Range 0-12

0-4 errors = Normal (DRS = 0)
5-6 errors = Borderline/Mild (DRS = 2)
7+ errors = Significant (DRS = 3)

Complete Worked Example:

Patient: 45-year-old male, 10th pass, referred post-stroke

VAIS: Info=15, Digit=8, Arith=6, Comp=14 → TQs: 88, 82, 80, 84 → VIQ = 84 (Low Average) → DRS = 2
Bhatia (Short): BD=8, PA=12 → Combined=20 → PIQ = 76 (Borderline) → DRS = 3
PGIMS: Total Raw = 62 → MQ = 72 (Mild Impairment) → DRS = 3
Nahor-Benson: Net Score 2.1 SD below mean → DRS = 3
BVMGT: 8 errors (Rotation, Perseveration, Fragmentation, Simplification, Angulation, Motor Incoordination, Closure, Collision) → DRS = 3

Combined DRS = 2 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 14
Interpretation: Severe brain dysfunction. VIQ > PIQ by 8 points (not significant but trending right hemisphere). All non-verbal and memory domains significantly impaired. BVMGT shows multiple organic signs. Pattern consistent with right MCA stroke affecting non-verbal, spatial, and memory functions.
