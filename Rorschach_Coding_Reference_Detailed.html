0. R and the core idea

R = total number of responses. Norm approx 17 to 27 (adult). R < 14 means invalid, readminister.

What it says: productivity and engagement. Very low R = guardedness, defensiveness, low energy, or limited resources; very high R = either rich productivity or an obsessive / over-inclusive style. Because many scores are counts, R must be considered when reading any frequency.

1. Location — WHERE the response was seen

CodeWhat it isWhat it says about the person
WWhole blotDrive to organize the whole picture; ambition / integration. Accurate W = good synthetic ability; many W with poor FQ = over-reaching beyond capacity.
DCommon detailPractical, conventional, economical focus on the obvious; reality-oriented everyday processing.
DdUnusual detailIdiosyncratic, peripheral attention; elevations = unusual scanning, over-focus on small things, perfectionism, or guardedness / avoidance of the whole.

W:D:Dd = the location ratio (processing approach). Typical emphasis is on D. Heavy Dd or heavy W shifts meaning as above.

3. Determinants — WHAT made it look that way (the engine)

Form

F = pure form. Drives Lambda = F / (R - F). Norm L approx 0.30 to 0.99.

Says: reliance on logic / structure and emotional distance. High F / high Lambda = avoidant, simplifying, narrows complexity and feeling. Very low Lambda = over-involved, takes in too much, easily flooded.

Movement (mental activity)

CodeWhat it isSays about person
MHuman movement = deliberate ideation, reasoning, fantasyInner mental life, imagination, capacity for thoughtful delay. M- = distorted thinking / judgement. M with no form = serious slippage.
FMAnimal movement = instinctual, need-driven ideationUrges / needs pressing on the mind; if high, intrudes on attention and concentration.
mInanimate movement = uncontrollable external forcesSituational stress, tension, sense of helplessness about outside pressures. Transient.
a / pActive vs passive coding of any movementFeeds a:p (flexibility of attitudes) and Ma:Mp (active vs passive / escape fantasy).

4. Form Quality (FQ) — accuracy of perception / reality testing

CodeMeaningSays about person
+Superior-overelaborated; precise + enrichedExact, high-quality perception.
oOrdinary; common and accurateSees what is really there, conventionally — sound reality testing.
uUnusual; uncommon but contours fitIndividualistic, unconventional, but not distorted.
-Minus; contours violated / distortedKey reality-testing failure — sees what is not really supported by the blot.
noneNo form usedPure colour / shading response without form control.
XA% = (FQ +, o, u responses) / R  |  norm > approx .89
WDA% = (W+D responses with FQ +, o, u) / (number of W+D)  |  norm > approx .85
X-% = FQ- / R  |  norm < approx .15 (>.20 problematic; >.29 serious)
X+% = (FQ + and o) / R  |  conventional accuracy
Xu% = FQu / R  |  individualism / nonconformity

Says: XA% / WDA% high and X-% low = reality testing intact. Low WDA% means distortion even in obvious areas = pervasive, serious. High Xu% with acceptable X-% = unconventional, not impaired. S- count localizes distortion to anger.

6. Popular responses (P)

P = count of the conventional "popular" answers (one occurring in at least 1 of 3 records). Norm approx 5 to 7.

Says: ability / willingness to see the world as others do. Low P = departs from convention (idiosyncratic, possibly impaired or oppositional); very high P = over-conventional, conforming, plays it safe.

7. Organizational Activity (Z, Zd)

Zf = number of responses earning a Z score = processing effort.
ZSum = total weighted Z; Zest = expected sum for that Zf.
Zd = ZSum - Zest = scanning efficiency. Norm approx -3.0 to +3.0.
Zd > +3.0Overincorporative: thorough, cautious, invests extra effort; can be inefficient / slow, perfectionistic.
Zd < -3.0Underincorporative: hasty, careless scanning; misses available cues, so faulty input, errors, impulsivity.
Zf lowLow effort / avoidant, or limited capacity.
PSVPerseveration, so cognitive rigidity / inflexibility (or processing difficulty).

9. Core ratios — Controls and Stress Tolerance

Lambda (L) = F / (R - F) · norm .30 to .99. >0.99 = avoidant (simplifies, narrows, sidesteps emotion).
EB = Sum M : WSumC, so coping style. Introversive (M dominant = thinks things through), Extratensive (colour dominant = feeling / trial-and-error), Ambitent (close = inconsistent, inefficient). Not interpretable if EA < 4.0.
EA = Sum M + WSumC, so available, organized resources for coping. Higher = more capacity. Norm approx 7 to 11.
EBPer = larger EB side / smaller side (when a style exists). 2.5 or more = pervasive (rigid, inflexible use of the style).
eb = (FM + m) : (SumC' + SumT + SumV + SumY), so demand / distress load. Right-side high = painful internalized affect.
es = FM + m + SumC' + SumT + SumV + SumY, so current stimulus demand. Adj es = es minus the excess m (all but 1) and excess Y (all but 1), so removes situational noise.
D Score = from (EA - es), banded: 0 plus or minus 2.5 gives 0; 2.6 to 5.0 gives plus or minus 1; 5.1 to 7.5 gives plus or minus 2 and so on, so it is the current capacity for control. Adj D = from (EA - Adj es) = trait / baseline capacity.

Says: Minus D / Adj D = chronically or currently overwhelmed, prone to impulsive lapses. D < Adj D = situational stress is currently lowering control. Positive D / Adj D = sturdy control.

AfrWillingness to engage emotion. Low (<.46) = avoids / withdraws from emotional stimulation; very high = drawn to / overstimulated by emotion.
FC : CF+CEmotional control vs discharge. FC>CF+C = well-modulated; CF+C dominant = under-modulated, intense; Pure C present = impulsive discharge.
Pure CRaw, formless emotion; impulsive affect.
SumC' : WSumCConstriction ratio: left side high = feelings suppressed / held in (painful internalization).
SNegativism / anger contributing to affect.
Blends:REmotional / psychological complexity.
CPDenial of negative affect (substitutes pleasant feeling).

EB (introversive side)Reliance on internal ideation for problem-solving.
a : p (active:passive movement)Flexibility of ideas / attitudes. Lopsided = rigid, fixed views. p > a+1 = passive ideational / interpersonal stance.

12. Self-Perception cluster

Egocentricity Index = [3 x (Fr + rF) + Sum(2)] / R. Norm approx .33 to .45.
3r+(2)/RAmount of self-focus. High (esp. with reflections) = self-absorbed / narcissistic; low (<.33) = poor self-esteem, regards self unfavourably.
Fr + rFReflections, so narcissistic self-regard, entitlement.
FDCapacity for balanced self-examination (adaptive); absent = little insight capacity.
Sum VPainful, self-critical introspection (guilt / shame).
An + XyBody / somatic preoccupation; concern about bodily integrity.
MORDamaged, "broken / spoiled" self-image.
H : (H)+Hd+(Hd)Self-concept based on real experience (H side) vs imagination / distortion (other side). Right side dominant = poorly reality-grounded identity.

GHR : PHRQuality of relationship representations; PHR>GHR = ineffective / off-putting relating.
SumTNeed for closeness. T=0 = distant / guarded; T>1 = neediness.
Human content total and Pure HInterest in and realistic perception of people. Low / no Pure H = limited, distorted social understanding.
a : pPassive interpersonal role if p dominant.
FdDependency on others.
PERDefensive, authoritarian, rigid relating; difficulty with non-submissive others.

Golden rules: (1) Never interpret one score alone — combine. (2) Compare to norms (deviation principle) but integrate into the person's whole pattern. (3) Frequencies depend on R. (4) Form = control; Movement = mental activity; Colour = emotion out; C' / shading = emotion / pain held in; Texture = closeness; Vista = painful self-look; Reflection = self-regard.
