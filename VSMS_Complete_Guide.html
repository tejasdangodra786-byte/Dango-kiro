🚨🚨 SPECIAL NOTE — CHRONOLOGICAL AGE (CA) MAXIMUM = 15 YEARS (180 MONTHS) 🚨🚨

The VSMS uses a MAXIMUM Chronological Age of 15 years (180 months) for ALL calculations.

If actual age is...CA used in calculationExample
Below 15 yearsUse ACTUAL age converted to monthsChild aged 8 years → CA = 96 months
Exactly 15 yearsUse 180 monthsAdolescent aged 15 years → CA = 180 months
Above 15 years (20, 30, 40, 50…)USE 180 MONTHS (cap)Adult aged 25 years → CA = 180 months (NOT 300)

WHY? The VSMS scale covers items only up to age 15 years. Maximum possible Social Age ≈ 180 months. Using actual CA above 180 would create artificially deflated scores.

SCORING METHODOLOGY — Item-Value System

How VSMS Scoring Works

Step 1: Mark each item Pass or Fail

MarkMeaningCredit
✓ or +Pass — child performs this habitually and independentlyFull (1 item)
½ or +FHalf credit — emerging / no opportunity but presumed capableHalf (0.5 item)
× or −Fail — does not performZero (0)

Step 2: Calculate Item Value for each Age Range

Item Value (months) = 12 ÷ Number of items in that age range

(Exception: 12–15 year range covers 36 months, so = 36 ÷ N)

Step 3: For each age range, calculate SA contribution

SA contribution (months) = Credited items in that range × Item Value

Credited items = full passes (count as 1) + half credits (count as 0.5)

Step 4: Sum all contributions = Total Social Age

Total SA (months) = Sum of SA contributions from every age range

Step 5: Calculate SQ

SQ = (SA in months ÷ CA in months) × 100

CA is ALWAYS in months. CA maximum = 180 months.
