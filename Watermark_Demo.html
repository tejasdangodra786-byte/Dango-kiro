Sample body text — try copying it

The Binet-Kamat Test is an individually administered age-scale intelligence test. Selecting and copying is fully allowed — but when you paste, you'll see "Made by Tejas Dangodra" woven all through the copied text (a header, a tag roughly every 12 words, and a footer).

Adjust the opacity slider toward 0.02 for almost-invisible, or toward 0.10 for clearly visible. My default subtle setting is 0.04 with about 4 marks on the page.
