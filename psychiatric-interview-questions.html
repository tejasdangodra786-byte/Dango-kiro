Psychiatric Interview Questions

Complete Clinician's Guide - All Questions for Every Section of Psychiatric History Taking (Based on Nitin Sethi Format)

1. Identification Data

−
Purpose: Establish rapport, gather demographic details, and assess reliability of the informant. Begin with open, non-threatening questions.

Basic Demographics

  • What is your full name?
  • How old are you? / What is your date of birth?
  • What is your gender?
  • What is your current marital status? (Single / Married / Divorced / Widowed / Separated)
  • What is your religion / faith / community?
  • What language do you primarily speak at home?
  • What is the highest level of education you have completed?
  • What is your current occupation? What work do you do?
  • Are you currently employed? If not, when did you last work?
  • What would you describe your family's socioeconomic status as? (Upper / Middle / Lower)
  • Where do you currently live? (Urban / Semi-urban / Rural)
  • What is your full current address?
  • Who has come with you today? What is their relationship to you?
  • Who knows you best and can give information about your illness?

Informant Assessment

  • How long have you known the patient?
  • Do you live with the patient? How often do you see them?
  • How well do you know the patient's daily routine and behavior?
  • Is there anything the patient might not want to share that you think is important for us to know?
Tip: Assess reliability by noting — does the informant give consistent details? Do they seem to exaggerate or minimize? Are they cooperative?

2. Chief Complaints

−
Purpose: Identify the primary reasons for seeking help in the patient's/informant's OWN words. Record duration for each complaint. List in chronological order of onset.

Eliciting Chief Complaints

  • What has brought you here today? / What is the main problem that made you come to see us?
  • Can you tell me in your own words what has been troubling you?
  • What are the main difficulties you have been facing recently?
  • Is there anything else that has been bothering you apart from what you just mentioned?
  • What would you say is the single biggest problem right now?

Duration & Onset for Each Complaint

  • When did this problem first start? How long have you been experiencing this?
  • Did it start suddenly one day, or did it come on slowly over time?
  • Can you remember the exact date or week when you first noticed it?
  • Was there anything happening in your life when this started? (Any stress, event, illness?)
  • Has this been getting worse, staying the same, or getting better since it started?
  • Is this problem present all the time, or does it come and go?

Intensity & Severity of Each Complaint

  • On a scale of 1 to 10, how severe would you rate this problem? (1 = very mild, 10 = worst imaginable)
  • How much does this problem interfere with your daily life and activities?
  • Does this problem stop you from doing things you normally do? Like what?
  • Has this problem affected your work / studies / household activities?
  • Has this problem affected your relationships with family or friends?
  • Has this problem affected your sleep, appetite, or self-care?

From the Informant

  • What changes have you noticed in the patient?
  • What is the main problem you have noticed? When did you first notice it?
  • How long has this been going on?
  • Is there anything the patient has not mentioned that you think is important?
  • What concerns you the most about the patient right now?

3. History of Present Illness (HOPI)

−
Purpose: Detailed chronological narrative of the current illness. Explore each symptom for onset, duration, intensity, course, progression, precipitating/aggravating/relieving factors, and functional impact.

Onset & Precipitating Factors

  • When exactly did you first start feeling unwell in this current episode?
  • Was the onset sudden (over days) or gradual (over weeks/months)?
  • What was happening in your life around the time the symptoms began?
  • Was there any stressful event — financial problems, relationship issues, job loss, bereavement, conflict, illness?
  • Were you physically unwell before the psychiatric symptoms started? Any fever, head injury, seizures?
  • Were you taking any new medications, drugs, or substances around that time?
  • Had you recently stopped any medications or substances?
  • Was there any change in your living situation, routine, or responsibilities?

Symptom Exploration — General Framework (Apply to EACH symptom)

For every symptom domain below, first establish PRESENCE or ABSENCE: "Have you experienced [symptom]? Yes or No." If YES, then ask all follow-up questions.
  • Presence: Have you experienced [specific symptom]? Is it present or absent?
  • Onset: When did this symptom first appear? Was it sudden or gradual?
  • Duration: How long have you had this symptom? Is it constant or intermittent?
  • Frequency: How often does it occur? (Every day? Several times a day? Weekly?)
  • Intensity/Severity: How severe is it on a scale of 1-10? How bad does it get at its worst?
  • Course: Has it been getting worse, better, or staying the same over time?
  • Aggravating factors: What makes it worse? Any situations, times of day, triggers?
  • Relieving factors: What makes it better? Anything that helps?
  • Associated symptoms: When this happens, do you notice anything else occurring at the same time?
  • Impact: How does this symptom affect your daily life, work, relationships, self-care?

Mood & Affect Symptoms

Depressed Mood

  • Have you been feeling sad, low, or down? (Present/Absent)
  • How long have you been feeling this way? (Duration)
  • Is the sadness there all day, or does it come and go?
  • On a scale of 1-10, how severe is the sadness at its worst? (Intensity)
  • Is there any particular time of day when you feel worse? (Morning/evening — diurnal variation)
  • Do you feel like crying? Do you actually cry? How often?
  • Do you feel hopeless — like things will never get better?
  • Do you feel worthless or like you are a burden to others?
  • Do you feel guilty about anything? What specifically?
  • Have you lost interest in things you used to enjoy? (Anhedonia — present/absent)
  • What activities have you stopped doing because of how you feel?

Elevated/Elated Mood

  • Have you been feeling unusually happy, elated, or on top of the world? (Present/Absent)
  • How long has this elevated mood lasted? (Duration)
  • Do others notice that you seem overly happy or excited? What do they say?
  • Do you feel like you have special powers, abilities, or importance?
  • Do you feel you need less sleep than usual but still feel energetic?
  • Have you been more talkative than usual? Do people say you talk too much?
  • Have you been spending more money than usual or making impulsive decisions?
  • Are your thoughts racing — like your mind is going very fast?
  • Have you been starting many new projects or activities?
  • Has this elevated mood caused any problems — at work, with family, financially?

Irritability

  • Have you been feeling more irritable or short-tempered than usual? (Present/Absent)
  • How long have you been feeling irritable? (Duration)
  • How intense is the irritability? Do small things make you very angry? (Intensity)
  • Do you lose your temper easily? How often?
  • Have you shouted at, threatened, or hit anyone because of your irritability?
  • What triggers your irritability? Any specific situations or people?
  • Can you control your anger, or does it feel out of control?

Anxiety

  • Have you been feeling anxious, nervous, or worried? (Present/Absent)
  • How long have you been feeling anxious? (Duration)
  • How severe is the anxiety — mild unease or severe panic? (Intensity, scale 1-10)
  • Is the anxiety about something specific, or is it general/free-floating?
  • Do you get sudden episodes of intense fear or panic? (Panic attacks — present/absent)
  • If panic attacks: How often do they occur? How long does each episode last? What symptoms do you get? (Palpitations, sweating, trembling, breathlessness, chest pain, dizziness, fear of dying)
  • Do you avoid certain places or situations because of anxiety? Which ones?
  • Do you have any specific fears or phobias? (Heights, closed spaces, crowds, social situations)
  • Do you worry excessively about many things? Can you control the worry?
  • Do you have physical symptoms with anxiety — trembling, sweating, palpitations, dry mouth, nausea?

Thought & Perception Symptoms

Delusions (Abnormal Beliefs)

  • Do you feel that people are against you or trying to harm you? (Persecution — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that people are talking about you, laughing at you, or referring to you? (Reference — present/absent)
  • Do you believe you have special powers, wealth, status, or a special mission? (Grandiosity — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that you have committed a great sin or done something terribly wrong? (Guilt — present/absent)
  • Do you believe something is wrong with your body — that it is rotting, diseased, or infested? (Somatic — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that your partner is being unfaithful? What makes you believe this? (Jealousy — present/absent)
  • Do you believe someone is in love with you? Who? (Erotomania — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that you or parts of the world don't exist? That everything is dead or gone? (Nihilistic — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that someone or something is controlling your thoughts, actions, or feelings? (Control — present/absent)
  • For each delusion present — When did this belief start? (Onset)
  • For each delusion present — How strongly do you believe this? Are you 100% certain? (Conviction)
  • For each delusion present — Is there any possibility you could be wrong about this?
  • For each delusion present — How does this belief affect your behavior? What have you done because of it?
  • For each delusion present — Is the belief interconnected with other beliefs? (Systematization)

Hallucinations (False Perceptions)

  • Do you hear voices or sounds that others cannot hear? (Auditory — present/absent)
  • If yes: How many voices? Male or female? Familiar or unfamiliar?
  • If yes: What do the voices say? Do they talk to you (2nd person) or about you (3rd person)?
  • If yes: Do the voices command you to do things? Have you obeyed?
  • If yes: Do the voices comment on what you are doing (running commentary)?
  • If yes: Where do the voices come from — inside your head or outside?
  • If yes: How often do you hear them? (Frequency) How long do they last each time? (Duration)
  • If yes: How loud are they? Can you ignore them? (Intensity)
  • If yes: When did you first start hearing them? (Onset)
  • If yes: Are they getting more frequent or louder over time? (Course)
  • Do you see things that others cannot see? (Visual — present/absent)
  • If yes: What do you see? (People, animals, shapes, colors, scenes?)
  • If yes: When do you see them — day or night? Eyes open or closed?
  • Do you smell things that others cannot smell? (Olfactory — present/absent)
  • Do you feel things on your skin — crawling, touching, burning — that aren't there? (Tactile — present/absent)
  • Do you taste things that aren't there or that others don't taste? (Gustatory — present/absent)

Thought Possession Abnormalities

  • Do you feel that thoughts are being put into your mind by someone else? (Thought insertion — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that your thoughts are being taken away or removed from your mind? (Thought withdrawal — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that others can hear your thoughts or know what you are thinking? (Thought broadcasting — present/absent)
  • Do you hear your own thoughts spoken aloud? (Thought echo — present/absent)
  • For each: When did this start? How often does it happen? How distressing is it?

Obsessions & Compulsions

  • Do you have repetitive thoughts that keep coming into your mind against your will? (Obsessions — present/absent)
  • If yes: What are these thoughts about? (Contamination, doubt, harm, symmetry, religious, sexual?)
  • If yes: How often do these thoughts come? (Frequency)
  • If yes: How distressing are they on a scale of 1-10? (Intensity)
  • If yes: Can you resist or stop these thoughts? What happens if you try?
  • If yes: Do you recognize these thoughts as your own? Do you know they are irrational?
  • Do you feel compelled to do certain actions repeatedly? (Compulsions — present/absent)
  • If yes: What do you feel compelled to do? (Washing, checking, counting, arranging, repeating?)
  • If yes: How many times do you repeat the action? How long does it take daily? (Duration)
  • If yes: What happens if you try to resist doing it? (Anxiety increases?)
  • If yes: Do you know that this behavior is excessive or unreasonable?
  • How long have you been having these obsessions/compulsions? (Duration of illness)

Biological Function Symptoms

Sleep

  • How is your sleep? Has there been any change in your sleep pattern? (Present/Absent)
  • What time do you go to bed? What time do you fall asleep?
  • How long does it take you to fall asleep? (Initial insomnia)
  • Do you wake up in the middle of the night? How many times? Can you go back to sleep? (Middle insomnia)
  • Do you wake up very early in the morning and cannot go back to sleep? What time? (Terminal/early morning insomnia)
  • How many total hours do you sleep per night?
  • Do you sleep excessively — more than 10-12 hours? (Hypersomnia — present/absent)
  • Do you feel rested after sleeping, or still tired?
  • Do you have nightmares or disturbing dreams? How often?
  • How long has your sleep been disturbed? (Duration)
  • How severe is the sleep problem — mild difficulty or cannot sleep at all? (Intensity)
  • What was your sleep like before this illness started? (Baseline)

Appetite & Weight

  • How is your appetite? Has it changed? (Increased/Decreased/Same — present/absent)
  • How many meals do you eat per day? How does this compare to before?
  • Do you have to force yourself to eat, or do you have no desire for food?
  • Or have you been eating much more than usual? Do you crave specific foods?
  • Have you gained or lost weight recently? How much? Over what period?
  • How long has your appetite been affected? (Duration)

Energy & Fatigue

  • How is your energy level? Do you feel tired or fatigued? (Present/Absent)
  • How severe is the tiredness — mild or completely exhausted? (Intensity)
  • Is the fatigue there all day from morning, or does it develop as the day goes on?
  • Has the fatigue affected your ability to do daily tasks — bathing, cooking, working?
  • How long have you been feeling this tired? (Duration)

Libido (Sexual Drive)

  • Has there been any change in your sexual interest or desire? (Increased/Decreased/Absent)
  • How long has this change been present? (Duration)
  • Has this caused any problems in your relationship?

Psychomotor Changes

  • Do you feel slowed down — like everything is in slow motion, including your thinking and movement? (Retardation — present/absent)
  • Or do you feel restless — unable to sit still, pacing, wringing hands? (Agitation — present/absent)
  • Have others noticed that you are moving or talking more slowly?
  • How long has this been present? (Duration)
  • How severe is it? Does it prevent you from completing tasks? (Intensity)

Diurnal Variation

  • Is there a time of day when you feel worse? Morning or evening?
  • Do your symptoms change throughout the day in a predictable pattern?

Cognitive Symptoms

  • Have you noticed any difficulty in concentrating or paying attention? (Present/Absent)
  • Can you read a newspaper article or watch a full TV program without your mind wandering?
  • Have you noticed any problems with your memory? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you forget recent events — like what you ate today or where you put things? (Recent memory)
  • Do you forget old memories — events from years ago? (Remote memory)
  • Have you felt confused or disoriented — not knowing where you are, what day it is, or who people are?
  • Have you had difficulty making decisions or thinking clearly?
  • How long have these cognitive difficulties been present? (Duration)
  • How severe are they — mild forgetfulness or seriously affecting daily function? (Intensity)
  • Are they getting worse over time? (Course)

Behavioral Symptoms

Aggression & Violence

  • Have you been aggressive or violent toward anyone? (Present/Absent)
  • What form did the aggression take — verbal (shouting, threatening) or physical (hitting, breaking things)?
  • How often have you been aggressive? (Frequency)
  • What triggers the aggression?
  • Have you injured anyone? Have you been arrested because of violent behavior?
  • Do you feel remorse after being aggressive?

Self-Harm & Suicidal Behavior

CRITICAL: These questions must always be asked. Ask directly and sensitively.
  • Have you felt that life is not worth living? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you wished you were dead or wished you could go to sleep and not wake up?
  • Have you had thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life? (Suicidal ideation — present/absent)
  • If yes: How often do these thoughts come? (Frequency)
  • If yes: How strong is the urge — a passing thought or an intense desire? (Intensity)
  • If yes: How long have you been having these thoughts? (Duration)
  • If yes: Have you thought about how you would do it? Do you have a plan? (Plan)
  • If yes: Have you made any preparations — collected pills, written a note, given away belongings?
  • If yes: What has stopped you from acting on these thoughts?
  • Have you ever attempted to end your life before? (Past attempts — present/absent)
  • If yes: When? What method? What happened? Were you hospitalized? What was your intent — did you want to die?
  • Have you harmed yourself without intending to die — cutting, burning, hitting yourself? (Self-harm — present/absent)
  • If yes: What do you do? How often? What triggers it? What does it do for you?
  • Do you have thoughts of harming others? (Homicidal ideation — present/absent)
  • If yes: Toward whom? Do you have a plan? How strong is the urge?

Social Withdrawal

  • Have you been withdrawing from family, friends, or social activities? (Present/Absent)
  • How long have you been isolating yourself? (Duration)
  • Do you stay in your room most of the day? Do you avoid meeting people?
  • Is this because you don't feel like socializing, or because you are afraid of something?

Decreased Self-Care

  • Have you been neglecting your personal hygiene — bathing, grooming, changing clothes? (Present/Absent)
  • How long has your self-care been affected? (Duration)
  • Are you able to cook, clean, or manage your daily routine?

Bizarre or Disorganized Behavior

  • Have you done anything that others found strange or unusual? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you been talking to yourself? Laughing or crying without reason?
  • Have you been collecting things, wandering, removing clothes in public, or doing things that don't make sense to others?
  • How long has this behavior been present? (Duration)

Course, Progression & Treatment

  • Overall, since the illness started, has it been continuous or does it come in episodes?
  • If episodic: How many episodes have there been? How long does each last? Do you recover completely between episodes?
  • Has the illness been getting progressively worse, staying stable, or improving?
  • Have you received any treatment for this current episode — medications, counseling, hospitalization?
  • If yes: What medications were prescribed? What dose? For how long? Did they help?
  • Did you experience any side effects from the medications?
  • Are you currently taking any psychiatric medication? Are you taking it regularly?
  • Have you had ECT (shock therapy) for this episode?
  • Have you been hospitalized for this episode? Where? For how long?
  • Why have you come for treatment now — what has changed or worsened?

Functional Impact

  • Has your illness affected your ability to work or study? In what way?
  • Has your illness affected your relationships — with spouse, children, parents, friends?
  • Has your illness affected your ability to care for yourself — eating, bathing, dressing?
  • Has your illness caused financial difficulties?
  • Has your illness affected your role in the family — as parent, spouse, earner?
  • Are you able to manage your household responsibilities?
  • Compared to before the illness, what percentage of your normal functioning would you say you are at now?

4. Past Psychiatric History

−
Purpose: Document all previous psychiatric episodes, treatments, and outcomes to understand the longitudinal course of illness.

Previous Episodes

  • Have you had any similar problems in the past before this current episode? (Present/Absent)
  • How many previous episodes have you had?
  • For each episode: When did it occur? (Year/approximate date)
  • For each episode: What symptoms did you have? Were they similar to now or different?
  • For each episode: How long did the episode last? (Duration)
  • For each episode: How severe was it? (Intensity)
  • For each episode: What was the diagnosis given at that time?
  • For each episode: What treatment did you receive — medications (names, doses), therapy, ECT?
  • For each episode: Did the treatment help? How much did you improve?
  • For each episode: Were you hospitalized? Where? For how long?
  • For each episode: Did you recover completely between episodes? Could you function normally?
  • For each episode: What triggered that episode? Was there a precipitating factor?
  • What was the longest period you remained well between episodes?
  • What was the shortest gap between episodes?
  • Have the episodes been getting worse over time, or are they similar each time?

Past Suicidal Behavior

  • Have you ever attempted suicide in the past? (Present/Absent)
  • If yes: How many times have you attempted?
  • For each attempt: When was it? What method did you use?
  • For each attempt: What was your intention — did you want to die?
  • For each attempt: Was it planned or impulsive?
  • For each attempt: How were you rescued? Did you seek help yourself?
  • For each attempt: Were you hospitalized? What treatment did you receive?
  • For each attempt: What were the circumstances — what was happening in your life?
  • For each attempt: How do you feel about that attempt now — regret, relief at surviving, or wish it had succeeded?

Treatment Adherence History

  • Have you ever stopped taking your psychiatric medications on your own? Why?
  • Have you ever stopped treatment because you felt better?
  • Have you ever stopped because of side effects? What side effects?
  • Have you ever missed follow-up appointments? Why?
  • What is your overall attitude toward psychiatric treatment and medications?

5. Past Medical & Surgical History

−
Purpose: Identify medical conditions that may cause or worsen psychiatric symptoms, and medications that may have psychiatric side effects.

Medical Illnesses

  • Do you have any medical conditions or physical illnesses? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have diabetes? (Present/Absent) If yes: Since when? How is it controlled?
  • Do you have high blood pressure (hypertension)? (Present/Absent) If yes: Since when? On medication?
  • Do you have any thyroid problems — overactive or underactive thyroid? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have epilepsy or seizures? (Present/Absent) If yes: Type, frequency, last seizure, medications?
  • Have you ever had a head injury or been unconscious? (Present/Absent) If yes: When? How severe? Any lasting effects?
  • Have you ever had a stroke (CVA)? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have heart disease or any cardiac problems? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have asthma or any lung disease? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have any liver or kidney disease? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you been tested for HIV? What was the result? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you had hepatitis B or C? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have any autoimmune disease — lupus, rheumatoid arthritis? (Present/Absent)
  • Do you have any neurological conditions — Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis, dementia? (Present/Absent)
  • Any other medical condition I haven't mentioned?
  • For each condition present: When was it diagnosed? What treatment are you on? Is it well-controlled?

Surgical History

  • Have you had any operations or surgeries in the past? (Present/Absent)
  • If yes: What surgery? When? Was there any complication?
  • Have you had any surgery on the brain or nervous system?
  • Did you have any psychiatric symptoms after any surgery? (Post-operative delirium, depression?)

Current Medications

  • What medications are you currently taking for your physical health?
  • For each: What is the name? What dose? How often? How long have you been taking it?
  • Are you taking steroids, beta-blockers, anti-epileptics, or any hormonal medications?
  • Have you noticed any mental changes since starting any medication?

Drug Allergies

  • Are you allergic to any medications? (Present/Absent)
  • If yes: Which medications? What kind of reaction did you have?
  • Have you ever had a severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to any drug?

6. Substance Use History

−
Purpose: Assess use of all substances — tobacco, alcohol, cannabis, opioids, sedatives, stimulants, inhalants, and others. For each substance, determine pattern, quantity, dependence, and complications.

Screening Questions (Ask for EACH Substance)

  • Do you smoke cigarettes, beedis, or use tobacco in any form (chewing, gutka, snuff)? (Tobacco — present/absent)
  • Do you drink alcohol? (Alcohol — present/absent)
  • Do you use cannabis/marijuana/ganja/bhang/charas? (Cannabis — present/absent)
  • Do you use any opioids — heroin, opium, morphine, codeine, painkillers like tramadol? (Opioids — present/absent)
  • Do you use sleeping pills or anti-anxiety medications (benzodiazepines) without prescription? (Sedatives — present/absent)
  • Do you use stimulants — amphetamines, cocaine, methamphetamine? (Stimulants — present/absent)
  • Do you use inhalants — glue, petrol, whitener, paint thinner? (Inhalants — present/absent)
  • Do you use any other substances or drugs that I haven't mentioned?

Detailed Assessment for Each Substance Used (Apply to ALL substances present)

Initiation & Pattern

  • At what age did you first try this substance?
  • What were the circumstances — peer pressure, curiosity, stress relief, experimentation?
  • How did the use progress from first use to current use?
  • What is your current pattern — daily, weekends, binge, occasional?
  • How much do you use in a typical day/session? (Quantity)
  • What route do you use — oral, smoking, injection, snorting, inhalation?
  • When did you last use this substance? (Last use)

Duration & Escalation

  • How long have you been using this substance regularly? (Total duration)
  • Has the amount you use increased over time? By how much?
  • Do you need to use more now to get the same effect compared to when you started? (Tolerance — present/absent)

Dependence Features

  • Do you feel a strong desire or craving to use the substance? (Craving — present/absent)
  • Do you find it difficult to control when you start, when you stop, or how much you use? (Impaired control — present/absent)
  • Do you need to take more of the substance to get the same effect as before? (Tolerance — present/absent)
  • Do you get withdrawal symptoms when you stop or reduce use? (Withdrawal — present/absent)
  • If yes: What withdrawal symptoms do you get? (Describe: tremors, sweating, anxiety, seizures, body ache, cramps, insomnia, etc.)
  • Have you neglected other activities because of substance use — work, family, hobbies? (Neglect of alternatives — present/absent)
  • Do you continue using despite knowing it is causing you physical or mental harm? (Continued use despite harm — present/absent)
  • Has your use become the most important thing in your life? (Salience — present/absent)
  • Has your variety of substances narrowed — do you use only one now? (Narrowing of repertoire — present/absent)

Complications

  • Has substance use caused any physical health problems? (Liver disease, gastritis, neuropathy, lung disease, injection-site infections, HIV risk?)
  • Has substance use caused any psychiatric problems? (Depression, psychosis, anxiety, memory problems?)
  • Has substance use caused social problems? (Family conflict, divorce, job loss, legal problems, accidents?)
  • Has substance use caused financial problems?
  • Have you ever been treated for substance use before? What treatment? Did it help?
  • Have you ever tried to quit? How many times? What was the longest you stayed abstinent?
  • What is your current motivation to quit — do you want to stop?

7. Family History

−
Purpose: Identify genetic predisposition, family dynamics, expressed emotions, and psychosocial stressors from the family environment.

Family Composition & Structure

  • What type of family do you live in — nuclear (parents + children) or joint/extended? (Type)
  • How many members are in your family? Who are they?
  • Tell me about each family member — their age, education, occupation, and health status.
  • Are your parents alive? If deceased, what was the cause and when?
  • How many siblings do you have? What are their ages and what do they do?
  • Are your parents together, separated, or divorced?
  • Who is the primary earner in the family?
  • What is the overall financial status of the family?

Family History of Psychiatric & Medical Illness

  • Has anyone in your family ever had a mental health problem? (Present/Absent)
  • Has anyone in your family had depression or mood problems? (Present/Absent) Who?
  • Has anyone in your family had psychosis — hearing voices, strange beliefs, behaving oddly? (Present/Absent) Who?
  • Has anyone in your family had anxiety problems or panic attacks? (Present/Absent) Who?
  • Has anyone in your family used alcohol or drugs excessively? (Substance use — present/absent) Who?
  • Has anyone in your family attempted or completed suicide? (Present/Absent) Who? What happened?
  • Has anyone in your family had intellectual disability or learning problems? (Present/Absent)
  • Has anyone in your family had epilepsy or seizures? (Present/Absent)
  • Has anyone in your family had dementia or Alzheimer's? (Present/Absent)
  • Has anyone in your family been hospitalized for mental health problems? (Present/Absent) Who? Where? What treatment?
  • For each family member with psychiatric illness: What was the diagnosis? What treatment did they receive? What was the outcome?
  • Is there any other mental or neurological illness that runs in your family?

Family Dynamics & Relationships

  • How would you describe the atmosphere in your family — warm, cold, conflictual, supportive?
  • How is your relationship with your father? (Close/Distant/Conflictual)
  • How is your relationship with your mother? (Close/Distant/Conflictual)
  • How is your relationship with your siblings? (Close/Distant/Conflictual)
  • How is your relationship with your spouse (if married)? (Close/Distant/Conflictual)
  • Is there frequent conflict or fighting in the family? About what?
  • Has there been any domestic violence in the family? (Present/Absent) Physical? Emotional? Sexual?
  • Who do you feel closest to in your family? Who do you go to for support?
  • Do you feel your family understands your illness? Are they supportive?
  • How has your illness affected your family?
  • Is there high expressed emotion in the family — criticism, hostility, or over-involvement?

8. Personal History

−
Purpose: Comprehensive developmental and life history from birth to present — identifying vulnerabilities, stressors, and personality development.

Birth & Early Development (Prenatal, Perinatal, Postnatal)

  • Were you a planned/wanted pregnancy? (Ask informant if possible)
  • Were there any complications during your mother's pregnancy? (Present/Absent) (Infections, toxemia, substance use, emotional stress?)
  • Were you born at full term or premature? (Duration of pregnancy)
  • Was the delivery normal (vaginal) or by cesarean section?
  • Were there any complications during birth — prolonged labor, instrument use, birth asphyxia? (Present/Absent)
  • Did you cry immediately at birth? (Birth cry — present/absent)
  • What was your birth weight? Was it normal?
  • Were you kept in incubator or NICU? (Present/Absent)
  • Were you breastfed or bottle-fed? For how long?

Developmental Milestones

  • Did you achieve your developmental milestones at the normal age? (Normal/Delayed)
  • When did you first sit, crawl, stand, and walk? (Motor milestones — normal/delayed)
  • When did you first speak words and sentences? (Language milestones — normal/delayed)
  • When were you toilet trained? Were there difficulties? (Normal/Delayed)
  • Were there any concerns about your development as a child?
  • Did you have any childhood illnesses — measles, meningitis, febrile seizures, head injuries? (Present/Absent)

Childhood Behavioral Problems

  • Did you have any behavioral problems as a child? (Present/Absent)
  • Did you suck your thumb or bite your nails? (Present/Absent) Until what age?
  • Did you wet the bed (enuresis) beyond the normal age? (Present/Absent) Until what age?
  • Did you have temper tantrums? (Present/Absent) How severe? How frequent?
  • Did you have night terrors or nightmares? (Present/Absent)
  • Did you have separation anxiety — fear of going to school or being away from parents? (Present/Absent)
  • Were you a hyperactive child — unable to sit still, always running around? (Present/Absent)
  • Did you have problems paying attention in class? (Present/Absent)
  • Were you a loner as a child, or did you have friends?
  • Were you ever bullied at school? (Present/Absent) Or did you bully others?
  • Did you ever experience physical, emotional, or sexual abuse as a child? (Present/Absent)
Tip: Ask about abuse sensitively. "Sometimes children go through difficult experiences with adults. Did anything like that ever happen to you?"

Educational History

  • What schools did you attend? How far did you study?
  • How was your academic performance — above average, average, below average?
  • Did you fail any classes or have to repeat a year? (Present/Absent)
  • Did you have any learning difficulties — reading, writing, math? (Present/Absent)
  • How did you get along with your classmates and teachers?
  • Were you involved in extracurricular activities — sports, arts, clubs?
  • Did you have any disciplinary problems at school — truancy, fights, suspensions? (Present/Absent)
  • Why did you stop studying? (If not completed education)
  • Did your mental health ever affect your education? (Present/Absent)

Occupational History

  • What jobs have you held in your life? List them from first to current.
  • For each job: What was the role? How long did you work there?
  • For each job: Why did you leave? (Fired, resigned, conflict, illness?)
  • For each job: How did you get along with your colleagues and supervisors?
  • Have you had difficulty keeping a job? (Present/Absent) Why?
  • What is your current work status? (Employed/Unemployed/Unable to work)
  • How satisfied are you with your current job?
  • Are there any work-related stressors currently?
  • What is your current financial situation? Any debts or financial stress?
  • Has your illness affected your work? (Present/Absent) In what way?

Sexual & Marital History

Psychosexual Development

  • How did you learn about sex? (From parents, peers, school, media?)
  • At what age did you reach puberty?
  • What is your sexual orientation? (Heterosexual/Homosexual/Bisexual/Uncertain)
  • Have you had romantic relationships before marriage? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you experienced any sexual abuse or trauma? (Present/Absent)

Marital History (if applicable)

  • Are you married? At what age did you marry?
  • Was it an arranged or love marriage? Did you have a choice?
  • How is your relationship with your spouse? (Good/Fair/Poor/Conflictual)
  • Are there frequent arguments or fights? About what? (Present/Absent)
  • Is there any domestic violence in your marriage? (Physical/Emotional/Sexual — present/absent)
  • Are you satisfied with your married life?
  • Have you or your spouse been unfaithful? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you been separated or divorced? (Present/Absent) What were the reasons?
  • Do you have children? How many? Ages? How is your relationship with them?
  • Are there any sexual problems in the marriage? (Present/Absent)
  • If yes: What is the problem — low desire, erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, pain, inability to orgasm?
  • How long has the sexual problem been present? (Duration)
  • Is the sexual problem related to your psychiatric illness?

Menstrual & Reproductive History (for Female Patients)

  • At what age did you get your first period (menarche)?
  • Are your periods regular or irregular? (Regular/Irregular)
  • How many days does your period last? What is the cycle length?
  • Do you have heavy or painful periods? (Present/Absent)
  • When was your last menstrual period (LMP)?
  • Is there any chance you could be pregnant currently?
  • Have you reached menopause? At what age? Any symptoms?
  • How many pregnancies have you had? (Gravida)
  • How many deliveries? (Parity) How many were normal? Cesarean?
  • Have you had any miscarriages or abortions? (Present/Absent)
  • Did you have any psychiatric symptoms during pregnancy or after delivery? (Postpartum depression/psychosis — present/absent)
  • Are you using any contraception? What method?
  • Do you have premenstrual mood changes — irritability, sadness before periods? (PMS — present/absent)

Legal History

  • Have you ever been in trouble with the law? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you ever been arrested? For what? (Present/Absent)
  • Have you ever been in prison/jail? (Present/Absent) For how long?
  • Are there any pending legal cases against you? (Present/Absent)
  • Is your current psychiatric condition related to any legal issues?

9. Premorbid Personality

−
Purpose: Understand the patient's personality BEFORE the onset of illness — their "usual self" when well. Ask the informant to describe how the patient was before they became ill. This helps distinguish personality traits from illness symptoms.

Temperament & General Disposition

  • Before this illness, how would you describe yourself as a person — what kind of person were you?
  • Were you generally a cheerful/happy person or a serious/gloomy person?
  • Were you an outgoing person (extroverted) or a quiet/reserved person (introverted)?
  • Did you prefer being around people or being alone?
  • Were you an optimistic or pessimistic person?
  • Were you generally calm and relaxed, or anxious and tense?
  • Were you an energetic person or a low-energy person?
  • How did you typically react to stress or difficulties? What was your coping style?

Interpersonal Relationships & Social Life

  • Before this illness, how many close friends did you have?
  • Were your friendships deep and lasting, or superficial?
  • Did you trust people easily, or were you suspicious/guarded?
  • How did you get along with others — were you easy to get along with or difficult?
  • Were you socially confident or shy and anxious in social situations?
  • Did you enjoy social gatherings and parties, or did you avoid them?
  • How were your relationships with authority figures — respectful, rebellious, submissive?

Interests, Hobbies & Activities

  • What hobbies or interests did you have before this illness?
  • Were you involved in any clubs, organizations, or community activities?
  • Did you enjoy sports, reading, music, arts, or any creative activities?
  • How did you typically spend your free time?

Moral, Religious & Ethical Attitudes

  • How religious or spiritual are you? Is religion important in your life?
  • What are your moral values — are you strict or flexible about rules?
  • Are you rigid in your beliefs, or open to different viewpoints?
  • Have your religious beliefs or moral values changed since becoming ill?

Predominant Mood & Emotional Patterns

  • Before this illness, what was your usual mood — happy, sad, anxious, irritable, stable?
  • Did your mood tend to fluctuate a lot, or was it generally stable?
  • How did you typically handle frustration? Did you get angry easily?
  • How did you react to disappointment or failure?
  • Were you an emotional/sensitive person or more logical/rational?
  • How did you handle stressful situations — did you cope well or break down?

Character Traits & Habits

  • Were you a responsible and reliable person?
  • Were you self-confident or did you doubt yourself a lot?
  • Were you independent or dependent on others?
  • Were you impulsive — acting without thinking — or careful and planned?
  • Were you a perfectionist? Were you orderly and organized?
  • Were you stubborn or flexible? Did you like things your own way?
  • Were you a risk-taker or cautious?
  • How did you make decisions — quickly or after much deliberation?
  • Were you truthful and honest, or did you tend to lie or manipulate?
  • Were you generous or stingy with money?
  • What were your eating and sleeping habits like before this illness?

10. Mental Status Examination (MSE)

−
Purpose: Systematic assessment of the patient's current mental state at the time of examination. This is the psychiatric equivalent of a physical examination. Some aspects are observed (appearance, behavior, affect), while others require direct questioning (mood, thought content, perception, cognition).

General Appearance & Behavior

Tip: Much of this section is OBSERVED rather than asked. Note your observations systematically. Some questions are directed to the patient to clarify observations.

Appearance (Observe)

  • What is the patient's build — thin, average, obese? (Observe)
  • Does the patient look their stated age, older, or younger? (Observe)
  • How is the patient's hygiene and grooming — clean, unkempt, malodorous? (Observe)
  • How is the patient dressed — appropriate, bizarre, disheveled? (Observe)
  • Are there any visible injuries, scars (especially self-harm scars), or physical abnormalities? (Observe)
  • Does the patient appear well-nourished or malnourished? (Observe)
  • Is there anything unusual about the patient's appearance that stands out? (Observe)

Psychomotor Activity (Observe & Ask)

  • Is the patient sitting still, or are they restless/agitated? (Observe)
  • Is the patient moving slowly — slowed movements, slowed speech? (Psychomotor retardation — observe)
  • Are there any abnormal movements — tremors, tics, mannerisms, stereotypies? (Observe)
  • Is the patient pacing, fidgeting, wringing hands, or unable to sit still? (Agitation — observe)
  • Are there any catatonic features — waxy flexibility, posturing, negativism, mutism? (Observe)
  • How is the patient's gait — normal, shuffling, unsteady? (Observe)

Attitude & Rapport

  • How is the patient's attitude — cooperative, hostile, guarded, suspicious, evasive, seductive, indifferent? (Observe)
  • Is the patient making eye contact? (Normal/Fleeting/Avoidant/Staring — observe)
  • Was rapport easy to establish or difficult? (Observe)
  • Does the patient seem interested in the interview, or disengaged? (Observe)
  • Is the patient answering questions willingly and fully, or being evasive? (Observe)

Speech

Tip: Assess speech characteristics throughout the interview by observation. Note rate, rhythm, volume, tone, spontaneity, and relevance.
  • Is the patient's speech rate normal, increased (pressure of speech), or decreased (poverty of speech)? (Observe)
  • Is the volume normal, too loud, or too soft/whispered? (Observe)
  • Is the tone normal, monotonous, or high-pitched? (Observe)
  • Is the speech fluent, hesitant, stuttering, or slurred (dysarthric)? (Observe)
  • Is the speech spontaneous or only in response to direct questions? (Observe)
  • Is the content relevant and goal-directed, or irrelevant and rambling? (Observe)
  • Is the speech coherent (can you understand what they are saying)? (Observe)
  • Are there any neologisms (made-up words)? (Observe)
  • Is there echolalia (repeating your words)? (Observe)
  • Is there mutism (not speaking at all)? (Observe)

Mood & Affect

Mood (Subjective — Ask the Patient)

  • How are you feeling right now, at this moment?
  • How would you describe your mood today? (In your own words)
  • If you had to pick one word to describe how you feel inside, what would it be?
  • On a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the worst you've ever felt and 10 is the best, where are you now?
  • Has your mood been the same over the past few days, or does it change?

Affect (Objective — Observe)

  • What is the predominant affect observed — euthymic, depressed, elated, anxious, irritable, flat, blunted? (Observe)
  • What is the range of affect — full range, restricted, blunted, or flat? (Observe)
  • Is the affect reactive — does it change in response to different topics? (Observe)
  • Is the affect congruent with the stated mood and thought content? (Observe — e.g., smiling while describing sadness = incongruent)
  • Is the affect labile — changing rapidly and unpredictably? (Observe)
  • Is the affect appropriate to the context of conversation? (Observe)

Thought — Form (Stream & Flow)

Tip: Thought form is assessed by how the patient speaks — the organization and flow of their thoughts. Listen to the pattern of their speech during the interview.
  • Is the patient's thinking goal-directed — do they answer questions in a logical, organized way? (Observe)
  • Is there flight of ideas — rapid jumping from topic to topic with loose connections? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is there loosening of associations — disconnected thoughts with no logical link? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is there tangentiality — going off on tangents and never answering the original question? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is there circumstantiality — eventually reaching the point but taking a very long, roundabout way? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is there thought blocking — sudden stopping mid-sentence with inability to continue? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is there perseveration — repeating the same word or phrase over and over? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Are there neologisms — made-up words? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is there word salad — completely incomprehensible speech? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Are there clang associations — words chosen by sound rather than meaning? (Present/Absent — observe)
  • Is the amount of thought increased (pressure) or decreased (poverty)? (Observe)

Thought — Content

Delusions (Ask Directly)

  • Do you have any beliefs that others might find unusual or hard to understand?
  • Do you feel anyone is trying to harm you, follow you, or plot against you? (Persecution — present/absent)
  • Do you feel people are talking about you or referring to you — on TV, radio, or in public? (Reference — present/absent)
  • Do you believe you have special powers, abilities, wealth, or a special mission from God? (Grandiosity — present/absent)
  • Do you feel you have done something terrible or committed a great sin? (Guilt — present/absent)
  • Do you believe something is wrong with your body — rotting, infested, not working? (Somatic — present/absent)
  • Do you believe your spouse/partner is unfaithful? What evidence do you have? (Jealousy — present/absent)
  • Do you believe that everything is hopeless, that you or the world don't exist? (Nihilistic — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that external forces are controlling your thoughts, feelings, or actions? (Control/passivity — present/absent)
  • For each delusion: How firmly do you believe this? Could you be wrong? (Conviction)
  • For each delusion: When did you start believing this? (Onset)
  • For each delusion: How has this belief affected your behavior?

Obsessions (Ask Directly)

  • Are there any thoughts that keep coming into your mind again and again that you cannot stop? (Present/Absent)
  • What are these thoughts about? How distressing are they?
  • Do you recognize them as your own thoughts? Do you know they are irrational?

Phobias (Ask Directly)

  • Are you extremely afraid of any specific thing or situation — beyond what is reasonable? (Present/Absent)
  • What are you afraid of? What happens when you encounter it?
  • Do you avoid situations because of fear? What do you avoid?

Suicidal & Homicidal Ideation (Ask Directly)

  • Right now, are you having any thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life? (Present/Absent)
  • Right now, are you having any thoughts of harming anyone else? (Present/Absent)
  • If present: How strong is the urge right now? Do you feel safe?

Overvalued Ideas

  • Is there any idea or belief that occupies your mind excessively — that you feel very strongly about? (Present/Absent)
  • Can you see other people's point of view about this, even if you disagree?

Thought — Possession

  • Do you feel that thoughts are being put into your mind from outside — that they are not your own? (Thought insertion — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that your thoughts are being taken away or removed from your mind? (Thought withdrawal — present/absent)
  • Do you feel that other people can know or hear your thoughts — that your thoughts are broadcast to the world? (Thought broadcasting — present/absent)
  • Do you hear your own thoughts spoken aloud, as if echoed? (Thought echo/Gedankenlautwerden — present/absent)

Perception

Hallucinations (Ask Directly for Each Modality)

  • Do you hear voices or sounds when there is nobody around? When nobody else can hear them? (Auditory hallucinations — present/absent)
  • If present: Is it one voice or multiple voices? Male or female? Familiar or unfamiliar?
  • If present: Do the voices speak to you directly (2nd person) or talk about you to each other (3rd person)?
  • If present: What do they say? Do they give you commands? Have you obeyed their commands?
  • If present: Do they comment on what you are doing as you do it? (Running commentary)
  • If present: Do the voices come from inside your head or from outside — a specific location?
  • If present: How loud are they — whispered, normal volume, shouting? (Intensity)
  • If present: How often do you hear them — constantly, several times daily, occasionally? (Frequency)
  • If present: How long do they last each time? (Duration of each episode)
  • If present: When did you first start hearing them? (Onset)
  • If present: Are they getting more or less frequent over time? (Course)
  • If present: Do they distress you? Do they make you do things?
  • Do you see things that others cannot see — people, shapes, shadows, animals? (Visual hallucinations — present/absent)
  • If present: What do you see? Is it clear and formed, or vague and shapeless?
  • If present: When do they occur — day/night? Eyes open/closed? Dark/light?
  • Do you feel things on your skin — touching, crawling, insects, electricity — that aren't there? (Tactile hallucinations — present/absent)
  • Do you smell things that others cannot smell — foul or unusual odors? (Olfactory hallucinations — present/absent)
  • Do you taste things in your mouth that shouldn't be there — poison, strange taste? (Gustatory hallucinations — present/absent)

Other Perceptual Disturbances

  • Do things around you sometimes look distorted — bigger, smaller, farther, or different than usual? (Illusions/distortions — present/absent)
  • Do you sometimes feel that you are unreal — detached from yourself, watching yourself from outside? (Depersonalization — present/absent)
  • Do you sometimes feel that the world around you is unreal — like a dream or a movie? (Derealization — present/absent)
  • If present: How often does this happen? How long does it last? How distressing is it?

Cognition

Consciousness & Orientation

  • Is the patient alert and awake? Or drowsy, confused, semiconscious? (Observe level of consciousness)
  • What is today's date? What day of the week is it? What month? What year? (Orientation to time)
  • Where are we right now? What is this place? What city are we in? What floor? (Orientation to place)
  • What is your name? Who am I? Who is the person who came with you? (Orientation to person)

Attention & Concentration

  • Can you count backwards from 20 to 1? (Serial subtraction test)
  • Can you subtract 7 from 100, then keep subtracting 7? (Serial 7s: 100, 93, 86, 79...)
  • Can you spell the word "WORLD" backwards? (W-O-R-L-D → D-L-R-O-W)
  • Can you name the days of the week backwards? (Saturday, Friday, Thursday...)
  • Can you name the months of the year backwards? (December, November, October...)
  • I'm going to say some numbers — please repeat them after me: 5-8-2, 6-9-4-7, 3-1-8-5-2 (Digit span forward)
  • Now repeat these numbers backwards: 2-4, 6-2-9, 3-9-1-4 (Digit span backward)

Memory

  • I'm going to tell you three words. Please remember them: "Apple, Table, Penny." Now repeat them. (Immediate recall/Registration)
  • After 5 minutes: Can you tell me the three words I asked you to remember? (Short-term/Recent memory)
  • What did you have for breakfast today? What did you do yesterday? (Recent memory)
  • When is your birthday? What year were you married? What school did you go to? (Remote/Long-term memory)
  • Who is the current Prime Minister/President? Who was before them? (General knowledge/Remote memory)
  • Can you tell me what happened in the news recently? (Recent memory + awareness)

Intelligence (Clinical Estimate)

  • What is your level of education and academic performance? (Background information)
  • How many states are there in India / countries in Europe? (General knowledge)
  • What is the capital of [country]? (General knowledge)
  • How far is it from [city A] to [city B]? (Estimation)
  • If you have 18 books and give away half, how many do you have? (Simple arithmetic)
  • If something costs Rs.80 and you pay with Rs.100, how much change do you get? (Practical arithmetic)

Abstract Thinking

  • What does this proverb mean: "A stitch in time saves nine"? (Proverb interpretation — abstract vs concrete?)
  • What does "People in glass houses shouldn't throw stones" mean?
  • What does "Don't cry over spilt milk" mean?
  • How are an apple and an orange similar? (Similarities — abstract: both fruits / concrete: both round)
  • How are a table and a chair similar?
  • How are a poem and a statue similar?
  • What is the difference between a lie and a mistake?
  • What is the difference between a child and a dwarf?

Judgment

Social Judgment (Practical Scenarios)

  • What would you do if you found a stamped, addressed envelope on the road? (Test judgment)
  • What would you do if you were in a crowded cinema and saw fire/smoke? (Test judgment)
  • What would you do if a stranger on the street asked you to keep their bag for them? (Test judgment)
  • What would you do if you saw a house on fire? (Test judgment)
  • How do you handle your money — can you manage your finances independently?
  • Are you currently making any important decisions — about marriage, money, job, property? Are these reasonable?

Personal Judgment

  • Do you think you are ill? (Assess awareness)
  • What do you think is causing your problems?
  • Do you think you need treatment? What kind of treatment?
  • What are your plans for the future — short-term and long-term?
  • Are these plans realistic given your current situation?

Insight

Insight is graded from Grade 1 (complete denial of illness) to Grade 6 (true emotional insight). Assess by asking the following questions:
  • Do you think there is anything wrong with you? Do you think you are ill? (Grade 1 if complete denial)
  • Do you think something might be wrong, but you're not sure what? (Grade 2 — slight awareness)
  • Do you think something is wrong, but it's caused by others/external factors — not an illness? (Grade 3 — aware but blames external)
  • Do you feel something is wrong with you but you don't know what exactly? (Grade 4 — aware something is wrong)
  • Do you think you have a mental illness? Do you understand what the illness is? (Grade 5 — intellectual insight)
  • Do you understand what caused your illness, what keeps it going, and what you need to do to get better? (Grade 6 — emotional insight)
  • Do you think you need treatment? Will you take medications regularly?
  • Do you think the symptoms you described are part of an illness, or do you think they are real/normal?
  • What do you think will happen if you don't take treatment?

11. Diagnosis & Formulation

−
Purpose: Integrate all information to arrive at a diagnosis and formulation. These are clinician-directed questions to guide diagnostic thinking.

Diagnostic Clarification Questions

  • Based on the symptoms described, do they meet criteria for a specific ICD-10/DSM-5 diagnosis?
  • Are the symptoms better explained by a medical condition or substance use?
  • Is the duration of symptoms sufficient to meet diagnostic criteria?
  • Is there significant distress or functional impairment (clinical significance criterion)?
  • Are there symptoms of more than one disorder (comorbidity)?
  • What differential diagnoses need to be considered and ruled out?

Formulation Questions (4 P's Framework)

Predisposing Factors

  • Is there a family history of similar illness (genetic vulnerability)?
  • Were there developmental problems, childhood adversity, or trauma?
  • Are there personality traits that increase vulnerability?
  • Were there early attachment difficulties?

Precipitating Factors

  • What triggered this current episode — life events, stressors, losses?
  • Was there a medical illness, substance use, or medication change preceding onset?
  • Was there a relationship breakdown, bereavement, or financial crisis?

Perpetuating Factors

  • What is maintaining the illness — ongoing stressors, poor support, non-adherence?
  • Are there maintaining cognitive patterns — negative thinking, avoidance?
  • Is there ongoing substance use worsening the condition?
  • Is the family environment maintaining the illness — high expressed emotion, enabling?

Protective Factors

  • What are the patient's strengths — intelligence, insight, motivation?
  • Is there good social support — family, friends, community?
  • Has the patient responded well to treatment in the past?
  • Are there good coping skills, religious faith, or other resources?

12. Treatment Plan

−
Purpose: Questions to guide treatment planning — biological, psychological, and social interventions.

Treatment History & Preferences

  • Have you had any treatment before for this condition? What worked and what didn't?
  • Are you willing to take medications? Do you have concerns about medications?
  • Have you had bad experiences with medications in the past — side effects?
  • Are you open to counseling/therapy? Have you had therapy before?
  • What are your expectations from treatment? What do you hope to achieve?
  • Are there any treatments you would refuse — ECT, hospitalization, specific medications?
  • Do you have access to follow-up care — can you come for regular appointments?
  • Can you afford medications? Is there financial constraint for treatment?
  • Who at home can supervise your medications and ensure you take them regularly?

Risk Assessment Questions

  • Do you feel safe right now? Is there any immediate danger to yourself or others?
  • Do you have access to means of self-harm — medications, sharp objects, pesticides, firearms?
  • Is there someone who can stay with you and ensure your safety?
  • Can we make a safety plan together — what will you do if you feel like harming yourself?
  • Are you willing to come to the emergency department if you feel unsafe?
  • Can I speak with your family member about keeping you safe?

Investigations Needed

  • Have you had any blood tests done recently? (CBC, LFT, KFT, Thyroid, Blood sugar)
  • Have you had any brain imaging — CT scan or MRI? (If indicated)
  • Have you had an EEG? (If seizures suspected)
  • Have you had psychological testing done? (IQ testing, personality assessment, neuropsychological testing)
  • Are there any medical investigations pending that might explain your symptoms?

Closing the Interview

  • Is there anything else you want to tell me that I haven't asked about?
  • Is there anything you're worried about that we haven't discussed?
  • Do you have any questions for me?
  • Is there anything you want to add that might help me understand your situation better?
  • Do you feel comfortable with the plan we've discussed?
  • Who should I contact if there's an emergency? What is their phone number?
